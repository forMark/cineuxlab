About the project

Designing better ways to discover stories.

CineUX Lab is an independent UX research project exploring digital experiences in cinema, entertainment and content platforms.

Mission

The goal of CineUX Lab is to understand how people interact with movie platforms and how thoughtful design can make discovery easier.

Research

Analyzing interface patterns, navigation systems and content structures.

Design

Exploring practical UX solutions for media products and blogs.

Learning

Documenting observations and sharing design insights.
